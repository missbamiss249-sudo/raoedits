This policy explains how RAO EDITS (https://raoedits.netlify.app) handles information.

Information we do not collect

RAO EDITS has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms. We do not ask for your name, photos or payment details, and the site does not upload your photos anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your theme, text size, language choice and saved prompts are stored in your browser using local storage. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it. You can remove it from the Settings tab or by clearing your browser data.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify may record technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to run the service. See Netlify's own privacy policy for details.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or www.aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

External links

This site links to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

RAO EDITS is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
